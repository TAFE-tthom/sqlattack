## Fixing An Order Mistake Again...

Unfortunately, when processing an order, another employee was logged into the terminal and a number of sales were made under employee ID 9. The employee who processed the orders was 'Laura Callahan'.

Use the information below to help with constructing your query. Use an `UPDATE` statement with your query and correct the entries.

* Employee
  * EmployeeID
  * LastName
  * FirstName
  * BirthDate
  * Photo
  * Notes

* Orders
  * OrderID
  * CustomerID
  * EmployeeID
  * OrderDate
  * ShipperID

* OrderDetails
  * OrderDetailID
  * OrderID
  * ProductID
  * Quantity
