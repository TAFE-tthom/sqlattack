## Fixing An Order Mistake

A single order for Customer ID 3 appears to have been a mistake, while it isn't known what customer actually ordered it, it is required to delete all order details related to the order they made.

The Northwind database is used in this particular question, you have access to the following tables. `DELETE` all order details where the orders where made by a customer with the `CustomerID` of 3.

* Customer
  * CustomerID
  * CustomerName
  * ContactName
  * Address
  * City
  * PostalCode
  * Country

* Orders
  * OrderID
  * CustomerID
  * EmployeeID
  * OrderDate
  * ShipperID

* OrderDetails
  * OrderDetailID
  * OrderID
  * ProductID
  * Quantity
